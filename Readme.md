# 👵 Grandma's Beef

A chaotic two-player fighting game where two grandmas settle their lifelong rivalry the only way they know how — by shooting body parts at each other.

Each player has a limited supply of "ammo" and must use it wisely to hit their opponent while dodging incoming attacks. Fight your way through different stages, survive until your opponent runs out of resources, and be the last grandma standing!

🎮 **[Play Grandma's Beef on itch.io](https://mayshvarts.itch.io/grandmasbeef)**

## 🎯 Gameplay

- Local two-player combat
- Shoot body parts at your opponent
- Limited ammunition
- Dodge and reposition to avoid incoming attacks
- Multiple stages
- Last grandma standing wins!

## ⌨️ Controls

### Player 1
- **W / S** — Move forward / backward
- **A / D** — Rotate
- **E** — Shoot

### Player 2
- **↑ / ↓** — Move forward / backward
- **← / →** — Rotate
- **Enter** — Shoot

A PlayStation controller is also supported.
